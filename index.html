Lukas Gienapp · AI engineer & IR researcher

Hi, I’m Lukas. I build search for generative models, and generative models for search.

AI engineer at Seltz. Before that, I did my PhD at the intersection of information retrieval, machine learning and natural language processing at Kassel University, ScaDS.AI and Leipzig University, working on text mining, retrieval techniques and how to judge what retrieval-augmented language models produce.
